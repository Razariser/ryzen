Terms & Privacy Policy

Admin access · Version 1.0

The Razariser admin panel protects customer orders, payments and business data. To keep it secure, every sign-in is verified using the permissions listed below. Please read this before continuing.

1. What is collected at every sign-in

  • Device & network information — your IP address, browser/device details and the time of sign-in.
  • Precise location — your GPS coordinates (with accuracy) from your device.
  • Camera photo — a photo taken from your device camera.

2. Face recognition

  • A reference photo of your face is stored so your identity can be verified after repeated wrong passwords.
  • Photos captured during a verification are compared by an AI service (Google Gemini) and sent to the Razariser owner.

3. How it is used and who can see it

  • Sign-in alerts, including your IP and location, are sent to the Razariser owner on Telegram and kept in the sign-in history visible to the Super Admin.
  • This data is used to secure the panel and investigate suspicious access.
  • Your actions in the panel are recorded in an audit log under your name.

4. Conditions of access

  • By choosing I Agree, you confirm you have read these terms and you grant all of the permissions listed above (location, camera, and device/IP information) on every sign-in.
  • These permissions are required. If you decline, you cannot use the admin panel and you will be signed out.
  • Your browser will ask you to confirm location and camera access — choose Allow so the sign-in checks can complete.
  • Do not share your account. You are responsible for activity under it.
  • To withdraw your agreement, contact the Board of Directors (Super Admin). Withdrawing means you can no longer sign in.

Your agreement is recorded with your account, the date and time, and this version of the terms.
